ScreenHelper AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

ScreenHelper AI helps you ask questions about content shown on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and which services help provide it.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and authentication data used to create and secure your account. Supabase provides authentication and account storage. If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares the identity information needed to sign you in.
  • Screen captures and questions: screen sharing starts only after you grant Android permission. A frame and any question you enter are sent when you tap “Get an answer.” The frame is processed by ScreenHelper’s server and sent to Coarena to generate a response. Coarena stores prompts and replies. Avoid including sensitive information in a shared screen or question.
  • Usage and payment records: account usage, answer credits, and payment status are processed to provide the trial and paid answer bundles. Lipwa processes M-Pesa checkouts; ScreenHelper stores payment references and settlement status needed to confirm purchases and prevent duplicate credits.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, answer questions about a screen frame you choose to share, manage answer credits and payments, maintain the service, and respond to support requests. ScreenHelper does not send a screen frame until you request an answer.

Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication and account data, Coarena for AI processing, and Lipwa for payment processing. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. Google processes sign-in when you choose Google as your login method.

Storage and retention

Account, usage, and payment records are kept as needed to operate the service and account. ScreenHelper sends screen frames, prompts, and replies to Coarena for processing; Coarena stores prompts and replies. The app may keep recent answer history on your device for the current session.

Your choices

You can stop Android screen sharing at any time and choose not to submit a frame. Do not share passwords, payment credentials, health information, or other sensitive content. To ask a question about your account or request account deletion, contact support using WhatsApp support.
